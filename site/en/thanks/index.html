Next: install it the first time

Nezzel doesn’t come from the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm before installing it. That’s expected, and you only allow it once.

  1. 1

    Tap “Download anyway”

    Your browser warns about any APK file from outside the store. The warning is normal. When the download finishes, tap “Open”

  2. 2

    Tap “Settings”, then turn on “Allow from this source”

    Android asks you to let your browser install unknown apps. Go back, then tap “Install”

  3. 3

    If Play Protect appears, let the scan finish

    Google Play Protect may offer to scan the app before installing it. Wait until it’s done, then open Nezzel
