Nezzel is an ad-free Android app for saving videos, audio and photos from supported public links. Share or paste a link, review the available options, then tap Download or Save.

Processing runs on your device, with Arabic and English support and no Nezzel account or tracking. Some links won’t work; private, sign-in-required and protected content aren’t supported.

Features
• Actual video and audio options returned by the engine. Unknown sizes stay unknown; estimates are labelled.
• Audio extraction and MP3 conversion.
• Public photo posts, photo selection for supported carousels, and original GIF image saves. GIF previews stay still.
• Download queue, cancellation, explicit retry, notifications, and saved-file Open, Share and Delete.
• Save to Download/Nezzel or a folder chosen through Android’s system picker.
• Arabic and English, RTL layout, light/dark themes and font scaling.

Tested link types
Completed saves on a Galaxy A34 include public video cases from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Snapchat Spotlight, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram, Dailymotion and Vimeo, plus direct MP4 links. Audio-only M4A and MP3 conversion were also saved from YouTube.

Photo saves passed for Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and a direct JPEG. An original LinkedIn GIF was saved and played with animation.

Options were inspected successfully for SoundCloud tracks, Mixcloud mixes, Bandcamp tracks, videos from Imgur, Odysee, 9GAG, Bilibili and Streamable, Twitch and Kick clips, and Telegram photo posts. Saving was not checked for these cases.

These are examples, not a promise for every URL. Rumble blocked requests in testing. Private, sign-in-required and DRM-protected content is outside scope.

Privacy
No ads, account, embedded browser, analytics or telemetry. Processing stays on your device; requests go directly to source sites/CDNs and upstream engine-update infrastructure. Automatic option loading may fetch metadata and previews while the app is in the foreground or accepts a shared/pasted link. Turn off Show options automatically for manual inspection. No background clipboard monitoring. Saving media always needs explicit confirmation.

Requirements and availability
Android 10+ and arm64-v8a only. Native 16 KB page-size compatibility is unresolved; current device evidence is for 4 KB pages. Store metadata does not announce availability on F-Droid or another store. Public APK distribution still needs matching corresponding source and complete third-party notices.
